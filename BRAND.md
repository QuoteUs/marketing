# QuoteUs brand guidelines

## Logo
Two lockups, in `brand/logos/`:

| Lockup | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark** (the Q) | Avatars, favicons, app icon, tight spaces |
| **Wordmark** (Q + "uoteus") | Headers, covers, press, anywhere there is room |

Colourways: **oxblood** (on light backgrounds), **blush** (on dark/oxblood
backgrounds), **ink** (mark only, single-colour print). A **dimensional** mark
exists for hero moments (SVG only).

Rules
- Never redraw, stretch, rotate, outline or set the logo in live text. Use the files.
- Keep clear space of at least half the mark's width on all sides.
- Minimum size: mark 16 px, wordmark 80 px wide.
- Oxblood on blush/white/ink-light; blush on oxblood/ink. No other pairings.

| Marigold | `#E9A83B` | Highlights |
| Paper | `#F6ECE6` | Light text on dark / warm backgrounds |

## Typography
- **Newsreader** (serif, variable + italic): headlines and quotes. Files in `fonts/`.
- Sans-serif system stack for UI and body text.

## Voice
Warm, short, a little playful. Talk about *friends, housemates and family*, not
"users". Lead with the moment ("the line nobody wants to forget"), not features.
Privacy is a selling point: shared only with the people who were there.
Dutch and English are both first-class; keep the two versions equally natural.
